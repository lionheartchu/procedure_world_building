## Project Concept

I am interested in exploring a 3D environment that grows and changes based on traces of everyday digital activity.

Different kinds of traces could become inputs that influence spatial form, atmosphere, movement, growth, or other environmental behaviors.

The exact input data and procedural systems are still open. I would like to use early experiments in the course to understand which parts of this idea are most appropriate for procedural world building and narrow the project direction from there.
